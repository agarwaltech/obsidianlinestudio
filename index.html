How it runs

From idea to final frame.

Phase 01

The Call

Thirty minutes. What the story is, who it's for, and what it has to do. You leave with a scope and a number — no proposal theatre.

Phase 02

World & Direction

Reference plates, look development and a shot list. You approve the world and the direction before a single frame gets generated.

Phase 03

Generation & Assembly

Shots generated, curated and cut. First assembly lands within 48 hours so you're reacting to something real, early.

Phase 04

Finish & Deliver

Grade, sound, score and export in every ratio you need. Two revision rounds included, masters handed over clean and organised.
